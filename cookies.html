Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Cookies are small files that websites store on your device. This page explains what our website does and does not use.

What we use

This website does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies of its own.

Links to other services

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps take you to their own websites and apps, which have their own cookie and privacy policies.

Changing your choice

  • Use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of our home page to allow or decline the map at any time.
  • You can also clear stored data or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not stop the website from working.
