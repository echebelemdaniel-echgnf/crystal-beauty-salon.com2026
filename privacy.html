Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how CRYSTAL Beauty Salon (“we”, “us”) handles your personal information when you use this website or book an appointment with us. We are based at 123, Riftaf Mall, beside Golf Estate, off Odili Road, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (“NDPA”).

1. What we collect

We only collect what we need to arrange your visit.

  • Booking form: your name, phone number, the service you want, your preferred stylist (optional), your preferred date and time, and any notes you choose to add.
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, phone call or Instagram, including your name or number and what you write.
  • Technical data that your browser sends to Google when it loads our fonts, and to Google Maps if you choose to load the map (for example your IP address and device details). See our Cookie Policy.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this website.

2. How the booking form works

The form on this site does not save your details on a server of ours. When you press “Send Booking via WhatsApp”, your device opens WhatsApp with a message pre-filled with the details you entered. Nothing is sent to us until you press send in WhatsApp.

3. Why we use your information

  • To arrange, confirm, change or cancel your appointment, and to contact you about it. We do this because you ask us to, and with your consent when you tick the box on the booking form.
  • To keep a record of bookings and services so we can run the salon and look after returning clients.
  • To answer questions you send us.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

4. Who sees it

  • Our team members who handle bookings.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), which carries your message to us. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.
  • Google, which provides our website fonts and, if you allow it, our map.

These services may process information outside Nigeria. We do not share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep booking messages for as long as we need them to look after your appointment and for our business records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to a particular use;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect what we did before you withdrew it).

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

7. Children

We offer styles for children. A parent or guardian should make the booking for anyone under 18, and the box on the booking form confirms this. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

8. Keeping information safe

We keep bookings in our WhatsApp and phone records, which are protected by the security of those apps and our devices. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send us anything you would not want others to see, such as card numbers or passwords. We never ask for these.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
