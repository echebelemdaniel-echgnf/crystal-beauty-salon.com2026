- Fully responsive (works on mobile, tablet & desktop)
- Image gallery
- Staff section
- SEO-ready (includes `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`)

## Before you go live

- Confirm the street number "123" in the address (index.html: hero, contact, footer, JSON-LD).
- Add a business email address if you have one (Privacy Policy and contact section) and your registered business name if you have one.
- Once you have your own domain, add a `sitemap.xml` and a `Sitemap:` line in `robots.txt`.
- The legal pages (`privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `refund-policy.html`, `cookies.html`) are plain-language drafts based on how this site works. Have them checked by a lawyer if you can, and edit the cancellation/refund wording to match your real salon policy.
- Make sure staff are happy for their name, photo and off day to appear on the site.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts. For maximum privacy, download them and host them in `css/` instead (this is disclosed in the Cookie Policy).
