Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

We want you to leave happy. This page explains how to change or cancel a booking, how deposits work, and what we do if you are not happy with a service.

1. Booking and payments

  • We do not take payments through this website. Sending the booking form does not charge you anything.
  • We may ask for a deposit to hold your booking. If we do, we will tell you the amount and how to pay when we confirm your appointment.
  • You pay the rest of the price for your service at the salon.

2. Deposits

  • If you cancel your appointment, your deposit is refundable, even if you tell us with less than 24 hours’ notice. To ask for your deposit back, contact us by phone or WhatsApp on +234 702 533 5401.
  • If you do not show up for your appointment and have not told us, we do not refund the deposit. Instead, we will move it to a new appointment date that suits you.

3. Cancelling or rescheduling

  • Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or move your appointment.
  • Call or message us on WhatsApp at +234 702 533 5401. We will gladly move your appointment to another time or day, depending on availability.
  • If you tell us with less than 24 hours’ notice, we may not be able to offer you a new slot straight away, but please still let us know so that we can help where we can.

4. If you are not happy with a service

  • Please tell us before you leave the salon, or contact us as soon as you can afterwards.
  • We will put the work right or adjust it for you.
  • We do not give refunds for services that have been carried out. Our aim is always to fix the problem.

5. Your legal rights

This policy does not take away any rights you have under Nigerian consumer protection law.
